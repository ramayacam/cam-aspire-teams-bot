# Invoicing Module (Aspire)

System URL: https://cloud.youraspire.com/

*Structure based on Aspire Academy Invoicing chapter (601–614), enriched with operational depth from the reference documentation. Covers the full invoicing lifecycle: from where invoices originate, through batching and sending, to receivables, payments, deposits, credit memos, and property-level settings.*

---

## What is Invoicing in Aspire?

An **invoice** is a bill sent to customers for completed work. The Invoicing module is where you prepare, review, send, track, and receive payment for invoices.

Potential invoices **originate from opportunities created at the property level.** Whether an invoice is available — and *when* — depends on the opportunity's invoice type (Contract vs. Work Order).

---

## The Invoicing Flow (left → right through the tabs)

The invoicing module's tabs are arranged to follow the lifecycle of an invoice from creation to accounting. Navigate to the module via the **paper-receipt icon** in the blue side panel (defaults to the Invoicing Assistant).

| Tab | Purpose in the flow |
|-----|---------|
| **Invoicing Assistant** | Front end for all opportunities/tickets that may need invoicing. Each line is a job **recommended** for invoicing — not an invoice yet. Flags issues (missing address, terms, tax) before you generate. |
| **Invoice Batches** | Holding area to review invoices for accuracy before sending. Statuses: **Not Complete** (pending) and **Completed**. |
| **Invoices** | Master list of all invoices generated (draft or sent). Draft = batch not completed. |
| **Receivables** | Current receivables aging by property; manage overdue balances, add payments, view credits. |
| **Payments** | Master list of payments and credit memos; used to create deposits. |
| **Deposits** | Master list of deposits. A deposit tells Aspire you're accepting revenue and depositing it into your outside accounting software (how accounting **recognizes revenue**). Statuses: **New** (created, not sent) and **Sent** (pushed to accounting). |
| **Log Event** | Log weather events (mostly snow) to attach to invoice batches. |
| **Electronic Payment Log** | List of electronic payments processed via Fiserv. |

> Monitoring these tabs consistently improves cash flow, reduces admin time, and keeps aging reports current.

## Invoice Types and When They Appear in the Invoicing Assistant

Potential invoices come from **opportunities at the property level.** The invoice type depends on whether the opportunity is a **Contract** (recurring) or **Work Order** (one-time), and it determines *when* the invoice becomes available.

⚠️ Invoice type is set during estimating and **cannot be changed without a change order.**

### Contract Invoice Types (recurring services)

| Type | Description | When it appears in Invoicing Assistant |
|------|-------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Fixed Payment** | Recurring invoice on a set billing schedule | Does **not** depend on ticket completion — generated by the time-of-month set at estimate. Same amount, same time each month. |
| **Per Service** | Invoice per completed service visit at agreed rate | After each work ticket is marked Complete. |
| **T&M (Time & Materials)** | Hourly rates + materials from completed tickets | On ticket completion; workers must enter time and materials first. |

### Work Order Invoice Types (one-time / enhancements)

| Type | Description | When it appears |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Fixed Price on Completion** | Single invoice for full WO amount | When all tickets on the work order are Complete. |
| **T&M on Completion** | Single invoice from time/materials across all tickets | Only when all tickets are Complete **and Approved**, with all entries in. |
| **Fixed Price Open Billing (FPOB)** | Bill any amount at any time, max flexibility | Always available; no thresholds. Invoiced by clicking into the row (no checkbox). |
| **Fixed Price on Payment Schedule** | Pre-agreed payment schedule (% complete) | Per the schedule defined on the opportunity. |

> **Note (subcontractor billing scope):** the requirement for subcontractor invoices to carry a PR + work ticket number applies only to **Work Order (extra work)** opportunities — **not** recurring **Contract** opportunities. See `subcontractor-extra-work-module.md`.

## Invoicing Assistant

The first tab and main entry point. Each row is a billing **recommendation**, not an invoice yet. It flags issues before you generate invoices.

### Quick filters
- Properties, Branches, Invoice Types
- **Invoice Timing** (first/middle/last of month — only affects Fixed Payment Contract invoices)
- **As of Date** — controls which items display by point in time. Set it into a target month to pull that month's Fixed Payment invoices. **T&M on Completion** appears when As Of Date ≥ the ticket's complete date; **Per Service** appears when service complete dates are ≤ the selected date.

### Status colors / values

The **Status** column indicates the action required (e.g., missing info) or that a line is ready to invoice. Click the **info icon** for status definitions; you can sort or group by status.

| Status | Meaning | Clicking row goes to... |
|--------|---------|-------------------------|
| Ready to be invoiced | Ready to batch | FPOB → Invoice Open Billing screen; others → no action |
| Ready and overdue | Ready, past due | Same as above |
| Can be invoiced | FPOB only | Invoice Open Billing screen |
| More work must be done | FP on Payment Schedule WO not ready | Nowhere |
| Address required | Billing address missing | Opportunity Invoice screen |
| Terms required | Payment terms missing | Property |
| Tax Jurisdiction missing | Tax setup missing | Property |

**When does a row have NO checkbox?** Three cases: (1) required information is missing, (2) a Fixed Price on Payment Schedule still has work remaining, or (3) always for **Fixed Price Open Billing** (invoiced by clicking into the row).

### Generating invoices
1. Filter the list, set As of Date.
2. Check rows in Ready/Overdue.
3. **Bulk Actions → Generate Invoice** → confirm date → Save.
4. One invoice generated per property (unless the property has **Separate Invoices** checked — see Property-Level Settings).
5. FPOB rows have no checkbox — click the row to invoice individually.

You can also build/group a working list via Display / Sort / Group (e.g., add Company, drag to group) and save it via three-dot → Save As.

### Permissions
- `View Invoice` — access tabs in Invoicing module
- `Create Invoice` — generate invoices via Bulk Actions
- `Edit Invoice` — complete batches

## Invoice Batches

The **Invoice Batches** tab is the holding area between creating invoices and sending them — review for accuracy first. Batch statuses: **Not Complete** (pending) and **Completed**.

### Creating a batch
From Invoicing Assistant → select Ready rows → Bulk Actions → Generate Invoice → confirm date → Save. Batch shows as **Not Complete**.

### Reviewing & editing
- Open batch → click an invoice → edit billing contact, invoice notes, payment terms → Save → browser back.
- ⚠️ **Do NOT adjust prices manually** except for T&M. All other invoice types must be adjusted via **change orders** — manual price changes corrupt end-of-month numbers.
- **T&M editing:** you can edit quantity, description, and unit price, and add/remove line items. Displayed quantities reflect **what was allocated to the ticket**; unit prices come from the **opportunity setup**. Changing a pending invoice changes only the overall invoice price — it does **not** update ticket costs or job costing.
- ⚠️ **T&M override case:** if a T&M override was applied during work-ticket approval, adjusting quantity or rates will **not** auto-update the total — you must manually edit the total to match the line items.

### Removing invoices / deleting a batch
- **Remove one invoice:** check it → Bulk Actions → Delete Selected Invoices. Returns it to Invoicing Assistant.
- **Delete whole batch:** three-dot menu → Delete Batch. All invoices return to Invoicing Assistant, and the batch number **refreshes to the next available** number.

### Printing
- Three-dot menu → Print All Invoices (Include or Exclude Paperless).
- Draft-batch prints carry a **DRAFT watermark**.

### Draft vs. Sent — what's editable
- **Draft** (batched, not sent): can add line items, change payment terms and invoice date. Prints with a Draft watermark.
- **Sent** (batch completed and sent): no line-item additions or invoice-screen changes; use Void and Reset or a credit memo instead.

⚠️ **Do not close a month with invoices still in Draft status** — complete or delete them first.

### Completing a batch & sending
1. Three-dot menu → **Complete Batch** → Confirm.
2. If the property has an Invoice Email contact, the Email Invoice screen opens.
3. Configure: Email message, Attach Layout, Include Proposal (single-invoice emails only), Add Attachment (applies to **all** invoices in the batch), recipient checkboxes. Uncheck any lines to skip emailing.
4. Click **Send**. Non-paperless invoices open a print window.
5. After completion, Aspire archives a PDF copy of each WO invoice as a property attachment (`InvoiceMMDDYY`).
6. For a paperless + email property, Aspire shows the **Email Summary** screen (no PDF pop-up) → Send.

### Batch screen — Related Event
Used for snow/weather events; description appears on all invoices in the batch (only events within 30 days available).

### 4-4-5 accounting period note
Companies on 4-4-5 calendars get checkboxes + Bulk Actions → Update Accounting Period (only on non-completed batches).

## T&M Invoices (special handling)

Contract **T&M** invoices as work tickets complete; **Work Order T&M on Completion** invoices only when *all* tickets on the opportunity are complete. T&M is the **only invoice type where adjusting revenue directly on the invoice is recommended** — for all other types, revenue changes must go through **change orders**, not the invoice screen. Revenue changes reflect in reports (P&L, end-of-month).

To edit: Invoicing Assistant → quick-filter T&M → select → Generate Invoice → confirm date → Save → drill into the invoice → adjust quantity/description/unit price, trash-can an erroneous item, or use **+** to add a line → Save → complete batch.

## Fixed Price Open Billing (FPOB) & Retainage

**Open Billing** is for long, unpredictable jobs needing varied progress billing (construction / design-build). It uses a **Schedule of Values (SOV)** defining how job elements appear on the invoice.

### Key behaviors
- FPOB opportunities are **invoiced individually — no checkbox, cannot be batched.** Click into the opportunity to invoice.
- Only **one** FPOB invoice can be created at a time.
- A Schedule of Values must be set up before an FPOB invoice can be created.
- The **amount field shows earned revenue as a *suggested* amount, not a cap.** It can go **negative** if invoiced exceeds earned — this does not block further invoices.
- SOV lines can be invoiced by **percentage** or by **dollar amount.**

### Schedule of Values (SOV)
Required on the estimate. Default config: Admin > Configuration > Application → "Default Schedule of Values to % vs. Dollars."

**Billing by dollar amount instead of %:**
1. Edit Schedule of Values on the estimate (during bidding or after).
2. **Switch the Quantity and Unit Price values** for lines you want to bill by dollar amount.
3. Lines with Quantity = 1 still behave as percentage-based.
4. When invoicing: enter quantity equal to dollar amount in **Amount This Invoice** (e.g., 6,000 to bill $6,000).
5. Percentage lines: enter decimal (.25 for 25%).

**To create an Open Billing invoice:** Invoicing Assistant → click the FPOB opportunity (no checkbox) → review top boxes (% complete; original + revision = total; earned vs. invoiced) → **New → Add Invoice** → on the SOV, enter % or $ per line → Save → confirm amount/date → Save → review pending batch → Complete Batch.

### Reverse and Rebill (FPOB only, closed months)
- Edit Invoice → 3-dot → Reverse and Rebill.
- Edit Amount This Invoice in the Invoice Schedule of Values screen.
- Voids original (e.g., 1234.001) and creates rebill (1234.002) in current period.
- Requires `Reverse and Rebill FPOB Invoice in Closed Months` permission. Excludes T&M services.

### Retainage (FPOB only)
Retainage = a portion of the price **held back as a completion guarantee** (typically 5–10%), released at project end or at milestones. Not required, but common. Invoiced **separately** via New.

**Setup:** On the work order with FPOB → Invoice Type → receipt icon → Invoicing Info → enter Retainage % → optional Retainage Maturity Date → Save.

**Workflow:**
- Invoicing Assistant → opportunity → New → enter amount → Aspire shows withheld retainage automatically.
- Pay Application layout: Page 2 shows accumulated retainage on Line 5; current due on Line 9.
- Invoicing for retainage: select **New** on the retainage line → enter date and amount (defaults to 100%) → Save.

**EOM treatment:** EOM Revenue tab includes a separate **Retainage** line (debit = withheld; credit = invoiced). In the Revenue Journal Entry, separate Retainage from Accounts Receivable → Retainage Receivable account on the balance sheet.

## Searching and Editing Completed Invoices

Locate invoices in the **Invoices** tab (check filters if an invoice seems missing). Useful display columns: **Invoice Batch Status** and **Email Status**.

### Draft vs. Sent — editing options

| Status | Meaning / what's editable |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Batched but not sent. Can add line items, change payment terms and invoice date. Prints with a Draft watermark. |
| **Sent** | Batch completed and sent. **No** line-item additions or invoice-screen changes. |

### Email/Batch Status combinations

| Batch | Email | Meaning | Action |
|-------|-------|---------|--------|
| Draft | N/A | Batch in progress, no email contact | Set invoice email contact if email needed |
| Draft | Pending | Has email contact, batch not completed | Complete the batch |
| Sent | In Queue | Batch complete, email queued | Wait; if stuck >1 hr, check user email sync |
| Sent | Sent | Email delivered | If not received, check spam, IT, sync, kickbacks |

### Edit Invoice screen — key actions (3-dot menu)

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Print Invoice | Choose layout + export type (PDF, Excel, HTML, PNG, MHT, RTF, Text) |
| Email Invoice | Opens Bulk Email Invoice screen |
| Publish Invoice in Customer Portal | Replaces last published invoice |
| Add Credit Memo | Only if invoice is Sent |
| Delete Invoice | Returns invoice to Invoicing Assistant. Works from **both Draft and Sent**, but only in an **open month** and only if **no payments are applied**. Deleting a sent invoice issues a **new invoice number** if regenerated. |
| Void and Reset | Returns a **sent** invoice to the Invoice Batches tab (back to Draft) while **keeping the same invoice number**. Use for description/billing-contact changes. Requires Modify Sent Invoices permission. |
| Process Electronic Payment | Requires Process Electronic Payments permission |
| Reverse and Rebill | FPOB only, in closed months. Voids original (1234.001), creates rebill (1234.002) |

⚠️ Never change dollar amounts from the Invoices screen (causes month-end over/under issues). Changing payment terms affects only that one invoice, not the recurring opportunity or property setup.

### Tax Jurisdiction override allowed?

| Type | Allowed |
|------|---------|
| WO FPOB / FP on Payment Schedule / WO T&M per Service | ❌ No |
| WO Fixed Price on Completion / WO T&M on Completion | ✅ Yes |
| Contract Fixed Payment / Per Service / T&M | ✅ Yes |

### Add Invoice Line Item options
Bounced Check, Clean up A/R, Finance Charges, Late Fees, Miscellaneous, Offset A/R Credit (Refund Customer).

## Receivables

The **Receivables** tab manages customers/properties/accounts — view open balances, aging, and credits **by property**, and create payments.

### Default lists
- **Open Balance** — all properties with outstanding balances.
- **Aging** — buckets: Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91+. Usable in filters (e.g., 61–90 > 0 for long-overdue).
- **Payments to Deposit** — payments not yet sent to accounting.

### Aging logic
Aging is based on the customer's **payment terms + invoice date** (not date sent). Example: 15-day terms → invoice within 15 days = Current; 16–45 days = 1–30 bucket.

### On Account / Credits
If the **On Account** field shows a number **in parentheses**, the property has a **credit** (appliable to any open-balance invoice via Add Payment). Unapplied credits continue to age until applied.

### Property showing twice in Receivables
If a property's Billing Contact was changed **without** checking "update open invoices to the new billing contact," the property appears **twice**: once under the old contact (old balance) and once under the new contact (new invoices). Always confirm the "update open invoices" prompt when changing a billing contact.

### Three-dot (per property) actions
| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Add Payment | Opens Payment screen prefilled |
| Send Email | Email billing contact (no statement attached) |
| Collection Notes | Add/view collection notes (communicate a balance to the team; show on property + A/R reports) |
| Print Statement | Single statement print modal (optional Include Invoices for combined PDF, max 50 invoices) |

### Permission
`View Accounts Receivable` — also grants Payments and Deposits tabs.

## Receiving Payments

Apply a customer payment through the Receivables tab.

### Key concepts
- **Payment Method** options: Check, Credit Card, Cash, EFT (typically ACH), or saved electronic methods.
- **Reference number** is **required** (e.g., check number) to locate the payment later.
- **Overpayment:** adjust the payment total in the Payment Summary; the extra saves as a credit to the account/property. **Underpayment:** change the amount on the invoice being paid; the invoice keeps a remaining open balance.
- **Payment Notes** stay on the payment only; **Collection Notes** also display on the property and on A/R reports.

> **Bot guardrail:** selecting **Credit Card or EFT does not actually process an electronic payment** in Aspire — that requires the Fiserv integration and a saved payment method.

### Applying a payment to an invoice
1. Receivables → 3-dot next to property → Add Payment.
2. Set Branch, Payment Method, Reference Number, Payment Date.
3. Check the invoice(s) to apply; Payment Summary populates.
4. Optionally apply available Credit.
5. Adjust totals for over/underpayment; add payment/collection notes → Save.

### Taking a payment without an invoice (prepayment / pay-ahead)
Customers can pay ahead on uninvoiced work; editing the **Payment Total** field creates a credit on the Regarding property/opportunity. Three ways:
- **Quick Menu → New Payment** → search contact/company → set Regarding to Property → Method, Reference, Date → edit Payment Total → Collection Notes → Save.
- **Payments tab → New → Add Payment** (same process).
- **Invoices tab → Invoices with Open Balance list → three-dot → add payment** directly to a specific invoice.

### Payment Screen — Key fields
- **Company / Contact / Property or Opportunity (Regarding)** — payment context (Property/Opp read-only after save).
- **Payment Date / Method / Reference No / Branch**
- **Payment Methods** — Check, Credit Card, Cash, EFT, or saved electronic methods (**only saved methods process via Fiserv**).
- **Invoices section** — checkbox to apply payment + Payment column for amount; **Fee column auto-populates for electronic** payments.
- **Credits section** — apply existing credit memos / unapplied credits.
- **Notes** — payment-level note (**hidden when an electronic method is selected**).

### Filters available on the Payment screen
Prefix (multi-select), Invoice # (multi-select), Description (string), Invoice Date (date), Invoice Amount (number), Open Balance (number).

⚠️ **Changing filters clears existing allocations** — set your filters before checking invoices to apply.

## Deposits

A **deposit** groups payments and sends them to your accounting software, which is how accounting **recognizes revenue.** Statuses: **New** (created, not sent) and **Sent** (sent to accounting).

Best practice: group payments by **payment type** using the **Payments to Deposit** list (excludes credit memos — those are handled via a month-end journal entry).

### Create a deposit
1. Invoicing → Payments → list dropdown → **Payments to Deposit** (groups by payment type, only undeposited).
2. Select checkboxes → Bulk Actions → Create Deposit.
3. Review Deposit Date / Status (defaults New) / Payments.
4. Trash icon next to a payment removes it (returns to Payments tab).
5. Save (return later) or **Send to Accounting** (3-dot menu) when ready.

### Notes & rules
- Accounting sync must be set up before **Send to Accounting** works.
- Credit memos are NOT included in deposits — handle via journal entry at month-end.
- Cannot delete a sent deposit or Reset to New in a **closed month**.
- Removing a payment from a deposit returns it to Payments to Deposit; deleting the whole deposit returns all payments to the Payments tab.

### Reset / Delete
- Sent deposit → 3-dot → **Reset to New** (only in open month) → modify → Send to Accounting.
- Unsent → 3-dot → Delete Deposit.

## Credit Memos

A **credit memo is a credit to a customer's account**, appliable to current or future invoices. Used for billing errors, returns, quality issues, cancellations, and discounts.

### Creation paths
- Quick Menu → New Credit Memo
- Payments tab → New → New Credit Memo
- Invoice screen → 3-dot → Add Credit Memo (against an existing invoice)
- Payments Search List → click credit memo line

### Two modes — division vs. Credit as Expense

| Setting | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| **Credit as Expense** ❌ (assign to division) | The credit is assigned to a **division** and treated as a **direct cost that reduces that division's revenue** (hits the P&L as reduced revenue). Appears as revenue impact on EOM. |
| **Credit as Expense** ✅ | The dropdown switches to **payment categories** (from Admin > Lists > Payment Categories) and the credit is recognized as a **company expense** (e.g., a discount) instead of reducing divisional revenue. Appears as an expense on EOM. |

**Rule of thumb:** assign to a **division** when it's a true revenue reduction for that service line; check **Credit as Expense** when it should be booked as a company expense (like a goodwill discount).

### Required fields
Billing Contact*, Credit Date*, Branch*, Billing State/Zip*, Property/Opportunity*, Division/Expense*.

### Tax fields
- **Sale Amount** = amount credited **excluding tax** (keep separate for accurate EOM).
- **Taxable Amount** = the portion subject to tax (can differ, e.g., $0 if tax-exempt).
- **Tax Amount** = auto-calculated from taxable dollars + jurisdiction; manual override limited to ±5%. Reflects in the **Tax Entity report**.

### Applying a CM to an invoice
1. Quick Menu → New Payment → select contact → Apply.
2. (Optional) Regarding to filter invoices.
3. Method = Check, Date, Reference Number.
4. Check the invoice; **Payment field auto-sets to 0** (because the CM amount applies, not a payment).
5. In Credits section, check the CM; full amount auto-applies — adjust if partial.
6. Verify Payment Summary minus credit → Save.

### Creating an invoice without an invoice present
Payments tab → New → New Credit Memo (or Quick Menu → New Credit Memo) → assign to contact/company → select branch → enter Credit Amount fields → Save (leaves a credit balance on the property).

### Crediting Sales Tax Only
1. Find invoice → 3-dot → Add Credit Memo.
2. Sale Amount = $0.00.
3. Taxable Amount = full taxable amount of the original invoice (not always = sale amount).
4. Tax Amount calculates automatically.
5. Apply → fill Property/Opportunity → Save.

## Miscellaneous Invoices

A **miscellaneous invoice** is tied only to a **property or customer** — not to an opportunity or work ticket. Created from Quick Menu → New Misc Invoice or Invoicing module → New.

### Reasons (item types)
- **Bounced Check** — record a returned/bounced check.
- **Clean up A/R** — apply an outstanding credit to a property to clean up aging.
- **Convenience Fee** — pass a card processing fee to the customer. Auto-created when the electronic-payment partner integration is enabled.
- **Finance Charges**
- **Late Fees**
- **Miscellaneous** — catch-all.
- **Offset A/R Credit / Refund Customer** — customer wants a different payment type or a cancellation refund.

### How to create
Quick Menu → New Miscellaneous Invoice → choose property → set payment terms (e.g., Due on Receipt) and tax jurisdiction → Add Invoice Line Item (e.g., Bounce Check, Late Fees) with amounts → invoice notes → Save (moves to Invoice Batches) → complete or delete.

⚠️ **Rules:**
- Created **one invoice and one property at a time** only.
- Should **not** be used to bypass the normal invoicing workflow.
- **Refund Customer** requires additional steps — ⚠️ **[VERIFY with AspireCare]** before relying on the bot for refund-specific procedures.

## Property-Level Invoice Settings

Three settings — two on the **property record**, one on a **contact** — change how invoices are created and delivered.

| Setting | Where | Effect |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **Separate Invoices** | Property record | Generates a **separate invoice per invoice type** available (driven by opportunity invoice type), instead of combining everything into one. A property can have multiple opportunities of different invoice types (recurring contract vs. one-time/extra), so this keeps each on its own invoice. |
| **Paperless Invoices** | Property record | Aspire does **not** generate a printable PDF on batch completion. Does **not** by itself email invoices — requires at least one Email Invoice contact. |
| **Email Invoices** | Contact on the property | Emails the invoice to that contact. Works even without paperless (customer gets an email; you can still print). |

**How to set:** Properties module → search property → three-dot → Edit Property → toggle Separate Invoices / Paperless Invoices → Save. For paperless, set an email contact: property contacts → three-dot on the contact → Edit Contact → check Email Invoice → Save.

⚠️ Tax jurisdiction and payment terms must be set on the property before invoicing — Aspire warns on a missing tax jurisdiction at invoice time. If you enable Paperless, ensure at least one Email Invoice contact exists.

## Electronic Payments (Fiserv Integration)

### Setup
1. Sign up via Fiserv partnership page or AspireCare ticket. Enroll for Credit Card, ACH, or both.
2. Wait for AspireCare to enable the integration.
3. Update user roles in Administration > User Management with permissions: `Process Electronic Payments`, `Void Electronic Payments`, `Refund Electronic Payments`, `Expiring Electronic Payment Method Alerts`, `Rejected Electronic Payment Alerts`.
4. Log out and back in.

After enabling: Customer Portal payment options activate; Electronic Payments tab appears in Configuration; Payment Methods tab appears on non-Employee/Sub contacts.

### Adding a payment method on a contact
1. Open contact (must NOT be type Employee or Sub).
2. Payment Methods → New.
3. Choose Credit or ACH/echeck.
   - Credit: Card #, CVV, Expiration. Only last 4 digits stored after save.
   - ACH: Routing\AccountNumber (backslash required: `123456789\987654321`).
4. Default Payment Method checkbox (at least one required).
5. Use Default Billing Information toggle → Save.

Adding during payment creation: same form + **Remember Card** checkbox (uncheck for one-time, e.g., gift cards).

### Processing electronic payments
Entry points: Quick Menu, Payments tab, Process Electronic Payments screen, Invoice screen Bulk Actions, Customer Portal.

⚠️ Don't select the generic "Credit Card" method — that doesn't process via Fiserv. Use a **saved** card.

**Bulk processing:** Quick Menu → Process Electronic Payments → filter by Payment Term (e.g., "Autopay") → check invoices, adjust amounts (partial allowed) → Bulk Actions → Process.

**Autopay workflow:** create a payment term named "Autopay" (Admin > Application > Lists > Payment Terms) → apply to properties → process via Process Electronic Payments + Payment Term filter. (Aspire has no fully automatic recurring billing — this is the closest.)

### Statuses
Pending (≤5 min typical) · Successful · Error (add Sync Error column for details).

The **Payments tab** also shows an Electronic Payment Status per row:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Success | Payment completed |
| Pending | Processor still working (typically a few minutes) |
| Error | Failed; check Sync Error column |
| N/A | Not electronic (cash/check/credit memo) |

Pending and Error rows are color-coded. Selecting a **Pending or Error** row opens the **Electronic Payment screen** (with Payment + Transaction History tabs); other rows open the regular Payment screen.

### Logs and receipts
- **Electronic Payment Log tab** — full transactional log including voided/expired records, error messages.
- Resend receipt: Log → select payment → Transaction History → 3-dot → Send/Resend Email Receipt.

### Void vs Refund

| Action | When | Result |
|--------|------|--------|
| Void | Before payment posts (~5 min window in Aspire) | Stops the transaction |
| Refund | After payment posts | Returns money to original card |

⚠️ Once a payment is sent on a deposit to accounting, the refund option is **locked** in Aspire — must use Fiserv directly.
⚠️ Fiserv void window in Aspire: until 8:30 PM CST same day (or following day if created after 8:30 PM).
⚠️ Fiserv does NOT allow partial voids/refunds.
⚠️ Fiserv does NOT notify Aspire of failed/uncollected ACH — check Fiserv portal or your bank.

### Refund Scenarios

| Scenario | Solution |
|----------|----------|
| Payment applied to wrong invoice (open month, deposited) | Reset deposit to New, delete deposit, edit payment allocation |
| Payment applied to wrong invoice (open month, not deposited) | Edit payment allocation directly |
| Payment to wrong invoice (closed month) | Misc Invoice (Clean Up A/R) on wrong client + CM (as Expense, Clean Up A/R) on correct client |
| Returned/Bounced/Declined | Misc Invoice with item "Bounced Check"; record refund account in accounting |
| Customer paid + requests refund (Aspire methods) | Send payment to accounting; cut check from accounting "Refund Customer" account; if no invoice, create Misc Invoice or CM |
| Customer credit balance + wants refund | Misc Invoice using "Offset A/R Credit (Customer Refund)"; apply credit; cut check in accounting |
| Fiserv: customer paid + needs refund | Refund directly in Fiserv; record entry in accounting |
| Fiserv: void before processing (Aspire) | Aspire → Payments → Void payment |
| Fiserv: void after sent to accounting | Void in Fiserv (not Aspire); create Misc Invoice "Clean Up A/R"; reprocess payment |

### Card Payment Compliance (PCI / Fiserv)
- Verify cardholder identity; collect CVV always.
- Process only legitimate goods/services tied to your approved business model.
- Refund only to original card; never to cash/ACH/different card.
- Never store card data in notebooks/spreadsheets/emails/screenshots.
- Use chip (EMV) → contactless → swipe (last resort) → manual key (avoid).
- Don't split transactions, run test charges, or process cards from owner/relatives.
- Use Fiserv-approved terminals/gateways; maintain PCI DSS.

> Aspire stores only the last 4 digits of cards. Fiserv handles all transactions and is PCI compliant.

**Alerts (require permissions):** Rejected Electronic Payments · Expiring Payment Methods.

## Prepayments / Deferred Revenue

Best practice: do NOT accept prepayments before the opportunity is created in Aspire.

### Two options to track in accounting
1. **With sales tax** — record prepayment, apply to invoice as a payment in Aspire.
2. **Without sales tax** — record prepayment, apply via Credit Memo in Aspire. Create payment category "Job Deposits" pointing to a Deferred Revenue account.

### Aspire-side options
- **Invoice full opportunity in first month** — apply prepayment + CM for any discount. Pro: eliminates A/R; Con: revenue recognized before earned, over/under report skewed.
- **Invoice monthly** — apply prepayment to property, allocate to monthly invoices. AR Aging shows credit balance until depleted.

### Recording the prepayment
Quick Menu → New Payment → set Regarding = Property (no opportunity yet) → Method, Reference, Contact, Branch, Date → Notes → Save.

### Tips
- Add Opportunity Tags like "2024 Contract Prepaid Customer" to batch separately.
- Use property notes to track deferred revenue balances.
- If the client cancels, follow the Refund Payments process.

## Aging & Statements

### Sending statements
- Receivables → check properties → Bulk Actions → Email Statement or Print Statement.
- Statements are per property, not per customer.
- Email available only when email sync is enabled. Sender: `notifications@youraspire.com`.

### Bulk Email Statement options
- To: Property Billing Contact / Email Invoice Contact / Primary Contact (multi-select).
- CC: contacts (all selected statements go to all CC'd).
- Include Invoice(s) checkbox: combines statement + invoices into single PDF (uses layout from most recently emailed invoice; max 50 invoices).
- Insert email tokens for dynamic data.

### Print Statement (bulk)
- Export Type: PDF, Excel, HTML, PNG, MHT, RTF, Text. (Without Choose Report Export Type permission, PDF only.)
- Include Invoice(s) is NOT available on the bulk Print modal.
- Standard or Balance Forward format depending on system config.

### Statement formats
- **Standard** — all invoices with outstanding balance.
- **Balance Forward** — prior balances rolled forward, current-month invoices listed.

## Weather Events (Snow Season)

Allows a weather event description to appear on all invoices in a batch.

### Setup
1. Admin → Application → Lists → Event Type → New (e.g., "Snow").
2. Invoicing → Log Event → New: Name, Branch (required), Event Type, Location, Start/End Date+Time, Zip Codes, Description, Attachments.
3. Zip Code field requires Branch + Start + End filled in first.
4. Pulls zip codes from properties with work tickets that have time/materials in the date range.

### Tying to a batch
- On Batch screen → **Related Event** dropdown (only events within 30 days available).
- Layout request needed for events to print on invoices (not default).

### Reporting
Weather Events Report (Reports → Standard Reports → Production) — group by property/zip; review tickets and invoices.

## Troubleshooting

### Customers not receiving invoices
1. **Invoices tab** → add display columns: Invoice Batch Status + Email Status.
2. Check the status combinations table above to identify the stage.
3. **Branch/Region settings** (User Settings → Administration → Organization) — verify Invoicing Email From + email sync.
4. **Configuration screen** — used when no Branch/Region set, OR when a batch spans multiple branches (Configuration takes priority for multi-branch batches).
5. **Contact record** — email must be unique and active (no duplicates, no leading/trailing spaces).
6. **User screen** — Aspire supports Microsoft 365 + Google Suite (others use SMTP via SPF/DNS). Auth tokens valid 90 days; refresh by logging in.
7. **Property** — verify Email Invoice + Primary/Billing checkboxes on contact.

### Lost / unmatched payments
1. Reports → Standard Reports → A/R Aging → Payment Source section. Note contact + amount.
2. Quick Menu → New Payment → search contact → Apply → match invoice + payment → Save.
3. **Deactivated contact** — Contacts module → remove Active filter → reactivate (Active radio button) → then apply the payment to the correct invoice.

## Common Questions (from Aspire Academy)

**Q: What is a credit memo?**
A credit to a customer's account that can be applied to current or future invoices — used for billing errors, returns, quality issues, cancellations, or discounts.

**Q: What does "Credit as Expense" do on a credit memo?**
Left unchecked, the credit is assigned to a division and reduces that division's revenue. Checked, it's assigned to a payment category and booked as a company expense (like a discount) instead of reducing divisional revenue.

**Q: Why isn't my invoice showing up in the Invoicing Assistant yet?**
It depends on the invoice type. Fixed Payment appears by the time-of-month set at estimate (independent of ticket completion). Per Service after each ticket completes. T&M on ticket completion with time/materials entered. Work Order types only when all tickets are complete (and approved, for T&M on Completion). FPOB is always available (click into the row).

**Q: What do the Invoicing Assistant status colors mean?**
They flag whether a line is ready or blocked — e.g., Address Required, Terms Required, Tax Jurisdiction Missing, or "More work must be done." Fix the flagged issue and the line becomes invoiceable.

**Q: Can I delete an invoice after it's been sent?**
Yes — from both Draft and Sent status, but only in an open month and only if no payments are applied. To only change a description/billing contact on a sent invoice, use Void and Reset (keeps the same number).

**Q: Why won't my T&M invoice total update when I change quantities?**
If a T&M override was applied during work-ticket approval, editing quantity or rates won't recalculate the total automatically — edit the total manually to match the line items.

**Q: What's the difference between Payment Notes and Collection Notes?**
Payment Notes stay on the payment record only. Collection Notes also display on the property and on A/R aging reports.

**Q: I selected Credit Card / EFT — why didn't it charge the card?**
Selecting Credit Card or EFT does not process an electronic payment on its own. Actual processing requires the Fiserv integration and a saved payment method.

**Q: How do I take a payment when there's no invoice yet?**
Quick Menu → New Payment → set Regarding to the property → enter method, reference, date, edit Payment Total → Save. Records a credit on the property for future invoices.

**Q: How does progress billing / Open Billing work?**
FPOB uses a Schedule of Values; invoice by % or $ per line, one FPOB invoice at a time (no batching). The amount field suggests earned revenue but isn't a cap.

**Q: How does retainage work?**
A portion of the price (typically 5–10%) held back as a completion guarantee, invoiced separately via New, released at project end or milestones.
